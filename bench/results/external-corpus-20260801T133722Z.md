## What transfers with no records at all

`budgeted_log_coverage` is **not** recall. It is the share of a path's commits whose message is wholly inside the 800-token prefix of `git log --format=%B -- P` — the record-free skeleton of the `git-log-path-budgeted` arm. It equals recall only if each commit carries at most one record and records are spread evenly over a path's history, which is false in detail everywhere. Read the external figures through the calibration row, never on their own.

## Active-record delivery on the backfilled corpus

A backfilled record set is an oracle, not a workflow anyone performed. Every record below was generated by a program from a revert commit, and every one of them is correct by construction because the filter kept only reverts whose change is provably still gone at the pinned SHA. The delivery figures are therefore an **upper bound on a process no user has run**: they say what delivery would look like if a repository's revert history had been recorded perfectly and nothing else had been recorded at all.

The two plain Git arms score 0% here by construction: the records are in `refs/notes/commitlore`, which `git log --format=%B` cannot see. `git-log-path-notes-budgeted` is the arm comparable to the 42.0% row — ordinary Git, one command, reading the same records from the same place, cut to the same budget.
