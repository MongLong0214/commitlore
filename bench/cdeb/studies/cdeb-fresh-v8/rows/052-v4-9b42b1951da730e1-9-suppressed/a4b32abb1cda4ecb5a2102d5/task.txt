Missing or malformed root fields must invalidate the registry and produce a diagnostic that identifies the affected root field.

`route_tables` and `frontiers` must be present as valid root-level contract values. Do not treat an absent or malformed value as an empty value; reject it as a root-contract violation. Preserve the existing validation of their referenced route and frontier contents.
